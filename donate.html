Laptop & School Supply Drive

Help give students the tools they need to learn, create, and explore. CuriousMindLab is collecting laptops, books, and school supplies to support students and families in our local community.

Donate an Item

Help Support a Student

Access to a computer and basic school supplies can make a meaningful difference in a student's ability to complete schoolwork, access educational resources, and explore new learning opportunities.

We have already begun collecting laptops and school supplies from friends and families, and we are now expanding the drive to our broader community.

What We're Collecting

💻 Laptops

Working laptops that can be used by students for schoolwork, learning, and educational programs.

Chargers should be included whenever possible.

📓 School Supplies

We welcome new school supplies including notebooks, pens, pencils, folders, binders, and other useful classroom materials.

📚 Books

Educational and age-appropriate books in good condition can also be donated.

How It Works

1. Tell Us What You Have

Complete our short donation form and let us know what you would like to contribute.

2. Coordinate With Us

We will contact you to arrange a convenient way to receive the donated items.

3. Support Our Community

We will work with community organizations to help direct donated resources to students and families who can make good use of them.

Laptop Donation Guidelines

Before donating a laptop, please make sure that:

  • The laptop is in working condition.
  • All personal files have been removed.
  • The device has been factory reset.
  • You have signed out of all personal accounts.
  • Activation locks and passwords have been removed.
  • The charger is included whenever possible.

If you are unsure whether your laptop is suitable for donation, feel free to contact us.

Ready to Donate?

Whether you have a laptop, a few notebooks, books, or other school supplies, your contribution can help provide students with resources they can use to learn and grow.
