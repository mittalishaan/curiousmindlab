Our Community

Learning grows through community.

CuriousMindLab works with local organizations, educators, volunteers, and community leaders to expand access to free educational opportunities for students.

We are grateful for the organizations and individuals who help us bring hands-on learning, enrichment, and educational resources to more students and families.

Community Collaborations

As CuriousMindLab grows, we are building relationships with schools, libraries, nonprofits, and other community organizations that share our commitment to education and opportunity.

Work With CuriousMindLab

Are you part of a school, library, nonprofit, or community organization? We would be happy to explore opportunities to provide free workshops, enrichment activities, educational resources, or other learning opportunities for your students and families.
